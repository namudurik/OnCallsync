# Customer Interview Script (30 minutes)

Goal: uncover current workflow pain and test willingness to pay.

## Before the call

- Confirm role (engineering manager, team lead, SRE lead)
- Confirm team size and on-call ownership
- Bring this script and a note template

## Opening (2 minutes)

"Thanks for taking time. I am researching on-call schedule operations for tech
teams using Google Calendar. This is a discovery call, not a product demo.
I will ask about your current process and pain points."

## Current workflow (8 minutes)

1. "Walk me through how your on-call schedule is created today."
2. "Which tools are involved from planning to publishing?"
3. "Who owns updates when someone swaps or takes PTO?"
4. "How often does the schedule change after it is published?"
5. "Where does the process break or become stressful?"

## Pain deep-dive (8 minutes)

1. "Tell me about the last time scheduling caused an incident or delay."
2. "What was the cost (time, missed handoff, escalation noise)?"
3. "How much manager or lead time is spent each month?"
4. "What is most annoying about your current setup?"
5. "If this problem disappeared, what would improve first?"

## Existing alternatives and constraints (5 minutes)

1. "Have you tried PagerDuty/Opsgenie/other tools for scheduling?"
2. "Why did you keep or reject those options?"
3. "Any policy/security constraints about tooling?"
4. "Must this live in Google Calendar, or can it be adjacent?"

## Willingness-to-pay test (5 minutes)

Use one direct ask:

"If we handled schedule generation and live swap updates directly in Google
Calendar and removed manual coordination, would you pay $X per team per month?"

Follow with:

- "At what price does this become a no-brainer?"
- "At what price is it too expensive?"
- "What approval process would this need?"

## Pilot close (2 minutes)

"I am opening a small pilot for teams with this exact pain. Would you like to
join if we can solve your top issue in the first month?"

If yes, capture:

- Pilot owner
- Timeline
- Budget range
- Success criteria
