# Pilot Offer and Outreach Templates

This document gives you concrete offers and messages to book calls and close
initial pilots.

## Core pilot offer

Use this exact structure:

- Target: engineering teams using Google Calendar manually for on-call
- Promise: remove manual scheduling, swaps, and handoff confusion
- Scope: one team, one rotation, one escalation path, Google Calendar only
- Pilot length: 2-4 weeks
- Pilot price test: choose one option per prospect
  - Option A: $250 setup + $99/month
  - Option B: $500 setup + $199/month
  - Option C: $1,000 setup + $299/month

## Offer positioning statement

"We set up and run your on-call schedule directly in Google Calendar, including
rotation logic and shift swaps, so your team stops managing coverage by hand."

## Cold outbound message templates

### Email / LinkedIn template 1

Subject: quick question on your on-call calendar workflow

Most are still handling swaps, holidays, and handoffs manually.

I am building OnCallsync to automate that workflow inside Google Calendar
without requiring a full migration to a new incident stack.

Would you be open to a 20-minute call this week? I can share what we are seeing
across teams and, if useful, propose a pilot setup for your schedule.

Thanks,
{{YourName}}

## Warm intro ask template

Hi {{MutualContact}},

I am validating OnCallsync for engineering teams that use Google Calendar for
on-call. Do you know 1-2 engineering managers or SRE leads dealing with manual
rotation changes or handoffs?

I am looking for short feedback calls and potential pilot partners.

## Call-to-pilot close script

At the end of a strong interview, ask:

1. "Based on what you shared, would removing {{top pain}} be worth paying for?"
2. "Would you be open to a 2-4 week pilot where we handle your current workflow?"
3. "For a pilot at {{price option}}, is there budget authority on your side?"
4. "If yes, what is the fastest path to starting?"

## Qualification checklist (before offering pilot)

Only offer pilots when all are true:

- They currently run on-call at least weekly
- They use Google Calendar in current process
- They report recurring manual changes or misses
- They can identify owner/budget for tooling
- Pain is current and active (not hypothetical)

## Weekly outreach operating targets

- 40 outbound messages/week
- 10+ positive responses/week
- 5 interviews/week
- 1-2 pilot asks/week

Track conversion in a simple sheet. Keep message variants and adjust weekly.
